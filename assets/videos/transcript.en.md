# Feature overview · English narration / bilingual subtitles

## 00:00 · A familiar moment after reading

You read the paper, but the title and method have faded. Just a few impressions remain.

读过的论文，标题和方法都模糊了。脑海里，只剩几个印象。

Something about agent optimization. Perhaps the OPD algorithm. Maybe a paper from a particular team.

好像是智能体的优化，似乎关于 OPD 算法，可能是某个团队出品。

## 00:13 · How Paper_Mind uses tags

Paper Mind describes one paper with several tags. Its research direction, method and team are all clues.

Paper Mind 用多个标签描述同一篇论文。研究方向、方法、团队，都是线索。

For example, agent optimization, OPD and a team label can all belong to this one paper.

比如，智能体优化、OPD、某个团队，三个标签可以同时属于这一篇论文。

## 00:29 · 01 · Save the paper and its clues

When saving a paper, keep its authors, year and venue. Missing details can be looked up.

所以，收藏时先保留作者、年份和出处，缺失的信息还能补查。

Give this paper several tags. This example is about both agent optimization and evaluation.

再给同一篇论文选上多个标签。这里既是智能体优化，也关注评估与可靠性。

Add one sentence about why this paper mattered to you.

再用一句话，留下你当时关注它的理由。

## 00:47 · 02 · Combine tags to find it again

Later, start with the idea you remember: agent optimization. It leads to an existing tag.

下次只记得智能体优化，就从这个概念找到已有标签。

That tag finds three related papers. Suggestions show which clue can narrow them further.

选中这个标签，先找到三篇相关论文。系统会提示还能怎样缩小范围。

Add evaluation. Only papers with both tags remain: two papers.

再选评估与可靠性，只看同时包含两个标签的论文，就剩两篇。

Search a remembered phrase. Your own sentence and matching notes make it clear: that is the one.

再搜一点记得的内容，看到当时那句话和命中笔记：对，就是这篇。

## 01:14 · 03 · Keep your reading organized

Keep commentary, saved article text and notes with the same paper, ready to revisit.

相关解读、剪藏正文和笔记，放在同一篇论文里，方便回看。

Track To read, Reading, Read and Revisit separately from your concept tags.

未读、在读、已读和待重读单独管理，不占用概念标签。

## 01:27 · 04 · Every tag has context

AI uses your tags and linked papers to explain each tag’s meaning and scope.

大模型根据你选的标签和关联论文生成说明，解释它的含义与适用范围。

## 01:33 · 05 · Find it, then keep exploring

Translate the abstract to return quickly to the paper’s main ideas.

摘要翻译，让你更快回到论文的核心内容。

Related papers are connected through shared tags, so you can keep following the idea.

相似论文按共享标签关联，帮你顺着线索继续阅读。

The map shows both sides: one tag can link many papers, and one paper can have several tags.

标签映射还能看到：一个标签关联多篇论文，一篇论文也有多个标签。

## 01:51 · 06 · Build a research direction

Topic packs use inclusion and exclusion tags to collect relevant papers automatically for your next reading group.

用包含和排除标签组成主题包，自动汇集相关论文，为下一次组会做准备。

## 02:00 · 07 · Use the models you prefer

Connect Claude Code or Codex through a local bridge, choosing your model and reasoning effort.

通过本机桥接接入 Claude Code 或 Codex，自选模型与思考强度。

Or connect a compatible model API you already use.

也可以接入你常用的兼容模型 API。

## 02:11 · 08 · Lightweight, with local control

No sign-up. Basic capture and search need no model, with bilingual UI and light or dark themes.

无需注册，基础收藏与查找无需模型，还支持双语和深浅主题。

Your library stays on your device, with import, export and automatic backups.

论文库留在本地，支持导入导出和自动备份。

## 02:25 · Paper_Mind

Paper Mind. Find your papers through the ideas you remember. Open source, lightweight, and ready for your next paper.

Paper Mind，通过脑海中的标签，找到你的论文。开源，轻量，从下一篇开始。
